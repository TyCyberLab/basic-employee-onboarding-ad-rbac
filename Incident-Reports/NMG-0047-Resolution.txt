Ticket Number: NMG-0047

Affected User: Jane Cooper (jcooper@NMG.com)

Issue Reported:
Jane Cooper was unable to access HR shared resources. Her desktop policies and mapped drives differed from her HR teammates.

Root Cause:
Jane Cooper’s account was incorrectly placed in the Operations Organizational Unit and assigned to the Operations-Users security group. Because Organizational Unit placement and security group membership control departmental policies and access permissions, she did not receive the HR policies and permissions required for her role.

Fix Applied:
- Moved Jane Cooper’s account from the Operations OU to the HR OU.
- Removed Jane Cooper from the Operations-Users security group.
- Added Jane Cooper to the HR-Users security group.

Verification:
- Confirmed that Jane Cooper appears in the HR OU.
- Confirmed that HR-Users appears in Jane Cooper’s Member Of list.
- Confirmed that Jane Cooper is listed as a member of the HR-Users security group.
- Confirmed that Jane Cooper now receives the correct HR department access.
